gwAnd is a native Android app for Gramps Web, the free web-based genealogy software. It shows and edits the family tree on your Gramps Web server – self-hosted or hosted. No account with the developer, no subscription, no ads.

Features:

* Tree: hourglass chart with ancestors, partners and descendants, pan and zoom freely; plus a fan chart across several generations. Bring any person to the centre with one tap, add relatives right from the "+" on a card
* Profile: the life as a timeline including marriage and births of children, family, photos, sources, associations and a map of places in the person's life
* Search across all people, plus places, sources, repositories and recent changes
* Editing: create and change people, events, family events, parents, partners and children; add citations and associations. Concurrent changes by others are detected instead of overwritten
* Photos: take or pick a photo and attach it to a person, set the main picture, photo overview of the whole tree with viewer
* Faces: the server's face detection suggests boxes, one tap assigns the person; drawing your own boxes works too
* Text recognition: let the server read document photos
* History: who changed what and when, undo single transactions; merge duplicate people
* Relationship between two people, PDF reports and export (e.g. GEDCOM) via the server
* Anniversaries: upcoming birthdays, wedding anniversaries and death anniversaries, optionally with a daily reminder
* AI assistant, if your server offers it
* Offline reading: recently viewed data stays available
* Compact on phones, two-pane on tablets; English, German, French, Dutch and Spanish

The app respects your account's role: guests can only view, members and editors can do more.

Requirements: a Gramps Web server with API 3.0 or later and an account on it. Face and text recognition, reports and the assistant depend on what the server has enabled.

Privacy: the app only talks to the server you enter. Stored on the device are the server address, user name and the sign-in token – never the password – and a read cache. No cloud backup, no analytics, no ads, no Google services. The map loads its tiles from OpenStreetMap.

gwAnd is not an official Gramps or Gramps Web project.
